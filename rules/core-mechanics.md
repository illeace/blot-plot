# Bot-Plot: Core Mechanics (Narrative d20)

## The Universal Stat Block
All characters are defined by six core stats (1-20):
- **Strength (STR)**: Physical power, athletics, and heavy tasks.
- **Dexterity (DEX)**: Agility, stealth, and fine motor skills.
- **Constitution (CON)**: Health, endurance, and physical resilience.
- **Intelligence (INT)**: Logic, knowledge, and analytical skills.
- **Wisdom (WIS)**: Perception, intuition, and willpower.
- **Charisma (CHA)**: Social influence, persuasion, and leadership.

## Narrative Checks (The d20 Roll)
When an action has an uncertain outcome, the LLM or a script resolves it by rolling a d20 and adding the character's stat bonus.

| Result (d20 + Bonus) | Outcome Description |
|----------------------|---------------------|
| **1-5**              | **Critical Failure**: The action fails spectacularly, with negative consequences. |
| **6-10**             | **Failure**: The action fails, or succeeds with a major setback. |
| **11-14**            | **Partial Success**: The action succeeds, but with a minor cost or complication. |
| **15-19**            | **Success**: The action is performed successfully. |
| **20+**              | **Critical Success**: The action is performed exceptionally well, often with an extra benefit. |

## Difficulty Classes (DC)
- **Very Easy (DC 5)**: Most people can do this effortlessly.
- **Easy (DC 10)**: A common task that requires some effort.
- **Moderate (DC 15)**: A challenging task for an average person.
- **Hard (DC 20)**: A task that requires significant skill or luck.
- **Epic (DC 25+)**: A near-impossible feat.

## Event Resolution
Events generated by the "Nightly Loop" should follow these rules to maintain consistency and tension in the story.
- **Outcome Persistence**: The results of checks should be permanently recorded in `memories.md` and `history/`.
- **Character Evolution**: Significant events or failures may lead to permanent stat changes, new traits, or even character death.
