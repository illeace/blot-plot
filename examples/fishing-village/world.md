# World: The Archipelago of Whispers

## Lore
A collection of islands across a treacherous strait from the sprawling metropolis of Lumen. The islands are known for their salt, rare sea glass, and independent-minded inhabitants.

## Geography
- **The Salt Isles**: Low-lying, rocky islands with sparse vegetation.
- **The Obsidian Peaks**: Volcanic islands to the south, home to rare minerals and hazardous terrain.

## Notable Factions
- **The Iron Sails**: A powerful guild of merchants and pirates who control the trade routes.
- **The Silent Monks**: A mysterious order that lives on the Obsidian Peaks and studies the ancient tides.

## Current Status
The archipelago is experiencing a period of unrest as the Iron Sails tighten their grip on trade, leading to food shortages and tensions in smaller fishing villages.

## Interaction Rules
- Magic is rare and often feared; people rely on their wits and resilience.
- The environment is harsh; every task is a struggle for survival.
- Social status is determined by one's contribution to the community and their relationship with the Iron Sails.
