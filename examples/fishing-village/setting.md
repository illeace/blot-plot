# Setting: Salt-Reach Village

## Description
A small, weathered fishing village on the eastern coast of the Salt Isles. The village is a cluster of wooden huts, fish-drying racks, and the "Barnacle Inn" that serves as the heart of the community.

## Notable Landmarks
- **The Barnacle Inn**: A cozy, if slightly damp, tavern frequented by local fishermen and traveling merchants.
- **The Obsidian Docks**: A series of obsidian-paved piers that are the only safe place to dock during a storm.
- **The Salt Pits**: A series of shallow pools where the villagers harvest sea salt during the summer months.

## Current Environment
The village is experiencing a particularly harsh winter, and the Iron Sails have recently increased their taxes on fish and salt, leading to a sense of unease.

## Key Personalities
- **Captain Elias**: The elder of the village, a retired sea captain who keeps order.
- **Old Mother Marla**: A local healer and herbalist who is said to have the gift of foresight.
