## Persona
- **Description**: A young woman with sun-bleached hair and calloused hands from years of fishing. She wears a worn leather apron and carries a small satchel filled with vials and herbs.
- **Personality**: Curious, observant, and determined. She is a quick learner and has a keen eye for detail.
- **Motivations**: To find a cure for her father's mystery illness and to escape the confines of Salt-Reach for a better life.
- **Background**: Born and raised in Salt-Reach, Elara spent her childhood on her father's boat. She recently discovered a hidden stash of alchemical texts in a shipwreck and has been secretly studying them ever since.

## Current State
- **Location**: Elara's family hut in Salt-Reach.
- **Condition**: Tired but determined. She has a small collection of herbs and a single, mostly-empty vial of an unknown liquid.
- **Goals**: To find a rare sea-flower that blooms only in the dead of winter, which she believes is the key to her father's cure.
