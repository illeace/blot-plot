## Persona
- **Description**: A young man with sharp features and a nervous energy. He wears a tattered, once-elegant cloak and has a small, ornate dagger at his belt.
- **Personality**: Adaptable, resourceful, and slightly paranoid. He is a quick thinker and a natural storyteller.
- **Motivations**: To escape the Iron Sails and start a new life in Salt-Reach.
- **Background**: Kael was a street urchin in Lumen who worked as a runner for a minor merchant house. He stole a valuable artifact from the Iron Sails and fled the city, eventually finding his way to Salt-Reach.

## Current State
- **Location**: The Barnacle Inn in Salt-Reach.
- **Condition**: Tired and on edge. He has a small stash of stolen coins and a few trinkets from Lumen.
- **Goals**: To find a safe place to hide from the Iron Sails and to find a way to earn a living in Salt-Reach.
